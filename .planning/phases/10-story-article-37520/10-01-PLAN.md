`ArticleFactory` (kind 37520, shipped Phase 8) that publishes/edits a Story while

editor panel and the proposal-accept republish (Plan 04) share one tested code path,
mirroring how Group inlined this logic in `GroupEditorPanel.handleSave`.
Output: `src/lib/nostr/story/{lifecycle,draft,index}.ts`, `lifecycle.test.ts`, and
`src/lib/hooks/useStories.ts`.
