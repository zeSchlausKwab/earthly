<objective>
Make the NIP-46 async/fallible settings load VISIBLE. Promote the sync hook's ref-based load
state into an observable `settingsStatus: 'idle'|'loading'|'loaded'|'failed'|'no-signer'` +
`settingsError` slice on the chat store, render loading / failed(+Retry) / loaded / no-signer
distinctly in the settings UI, and drive Retry through a store nonce that re-enters the
existing generation-counter guard (never calling the loader directly).

Purpose: SET-02 requires the remote-signer decrypt path to fail VISIBLY rather than silently
appearing as data loss. Today the hook applies defaults + a single toast on failure, which is
indistinguishable from a fresh user. This closes that gap (D-11) while preserving the
stale-load race protection (Pitfall 2) and the in-memory-only no-signer behavior (D-12).

Output: a `settingsStatus`/`settingsError`/`settingsLoadNonce` slice + `setSettingsStatus` and
`requestSettingsReload` actions in `store.ts`; amended load lifecycle in `useChatSettingsSync.ts`;
a load-state banner in `ChatSettingsSection.tsx`.
</objective>
