How the flip works
01
One class, no forksAdd .earthly-light (or shadcn’s .light) on a container and every child re-reads the same aliases — surfaces, borders, text — from paper values.
02
Borders replace glowOn dark, hairlines are lighter than the surface; on light they’re darker. The border-first elevation model means shadows stay minimal in both.
03
Where light fitsDark is the working Studio (long map sessions, less glare against imagery). Light suits print/export, embedded docs, and daytime mobile capture. Same components, user or context picks.

The four space savings
Color shares the name rowA 26px swatch button sits to the right of the name field — one click opens the picker popover. Saves a whole labeled row.
Geometries expandCollapsed they’re 24px rows; expand one to reveal type, validity, and its own properties. Fly-to + delete on the row.

TURN 4 · 4a Design system · edgier + denser

From soft & round to a dense instrument.

The mobile sketches read friendly but spendthrift — big radii, fat margins, roomy type. This evolves the radix-mira base toward a professional-tool language: the economy of Blender or 3ds Max, where a screen has to hold a lot and every pixel is accounted for. Sharp corners, hairline borders instead of shadows, a tight grid, and small-but-legible type. Not a clone of those apps — their discipline, in Earthly’s palette.
